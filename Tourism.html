    Maletsunyane Falls

    Thaba-Bosiu National Monument

    A towering 192-meter single-drop waterfall located in Semonkong
    Famous for hosting a world-record commercial abseil down the cliff face.

    The historic sandstone plateau fortress that served as the cradle
    of the Basotho nation under King Moshoeshoe I.

    Katse Dam

    Afriski

    A massive engineering marvel in the Maluti Mountains
    offering boat tours and alpine flora displays.

    This is the only commercial ski resort in Sub-Saharan Africa
    drawing winter sports enthusiasts for skiing and snowboarding.

    Morija Museum & archives

    Maliba Lodge is the first and only 5-star ecolodge in Lesotho,Southern Africa
    and is located in the pristine Tsehlanyane National Park.

    The country's oldest cultural museum, hosting historical artifacts
    and hosting annual arts festival.
